About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Lamb Surface Processes Group within the GPS Division at the California Institute of Technology. My research advances fundamental theories in Environmental Fluid Mechanics and Sediment Transport, with implications for water-resource management, coastal resilience, and planetary science.

I integrate high-performance computing models, remote sensing, and machine learning to address complex environmental challenges in both built and natural systems. With expertise in numerical modeling of multiphase flows and riverine–coastal processes, I utilize low- and high-order solvers such as NEK5000, OpenFOAM, and OpenTelemac to provide actionable insights for water-resource management and tail-event risk mitigation.
