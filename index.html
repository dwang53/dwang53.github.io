About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Lamb Surface Processes Group within the GPS division at the California Institute of Technology. My research advances fundamental theories in Environmental Fluid Mechanics and Sediment Transport.

I integrate high-performance computing models, remote sensing, and machine learning to address complex environmental challenges in both built and natural systems. With expertise in high-performance numerical modeling of multiphase flows and riverine–coastal processes, I utilize low- and high-order solvers such as NEK5000, OpenFOAM, and OpenTelemac to provide actionable insights for water-resources management and tail-event risk mitigation.
