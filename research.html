Selected Research Projects

NASA Delta-X

Numerical modeling of the Mississippi River Delta’s land evolution and climate risk assessment. Enabling deltas to thrive in a century of rising seas, in collaboration with NASA JPL, BU, and UT Austin.

Freshwater Focculation

Impact of flocculation on sustaining floodplains and deltaic wetlands, in collaboration with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and Water Institute. (Funding: NSF)

Great Lakes Watershed Climate Change

Impact of climate change and invasive species mitigation on the Chicago Area Waterway System, relating to flooding, water quality, and navigation. (Funding: NSF and MWRD)

Extreme Weather Mudflow/Debris Flow

Simulation of mudflow/debris flow during a 1,000-year precipitation event in Palmdale, CA: climate-related extreme weather and safety/risk analysis. (Funding: Private sector)

Density-Driven Mixing Layers

Investigation of mixing layers and surface patterns in the Chicago Sanitary and Ship Canal, in collaboration with the U.S. Geological Survey.

Large Wood Debris (LWD) Impact

Quantifying the effects of LWD patches on flow resistance and bank erosion in evolving meandering rivers, in collaboration with the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
